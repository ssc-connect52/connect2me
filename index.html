How to Install

4 steps
  1. Download the APK Tap Download the APK at the top of this page.
  2. Allow installs if prompted If Android blocks the file, allow installs from your browser or file manager in your phone settings.
  3. Open the APK file Once the download finishes, open it and tap Install.
  4. Launch SSC Connect Open the app and continue with account registration or login.

Before You Start

Three things to check before you register.

Use your institutional email Only @msusnd.edu.ph accounts can register and verify.
Internet is required Needed for sign-up, email verification, login, and notification setup.
Enable notifications So you receive announcements, event updates, and alerts in real time.
